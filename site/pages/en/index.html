What's inside

✨

Assistant

Finds out what works for you. If "Minecraft" does 3.5 times better than the rest, you get ready-made titles, the right format and your best time slot.

🗓️

Calendar & queue

Month and week view, reschedule by dragging, fixed time slots. Subscribe in Apple Calendar and Outlook – it updates itself.

✍️

Composer

One text, every channel: preview and character limit per platform, custom versions and a text check against the most common reach killers.

🚀

Automatic publishing

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and X: pick a video, set a time, done. Interrupted uploads resume where they stopped.

📈

Analytics & Coach

Trends, comparison with the previous period, best weekday and time – plus 19 rules that turn them into concrete next steps.

🔌

Connections

YouTube with just your channel name, Twitch by signing in. New videos, streams, clips and followers come in every 20 minutes.

💡

Ideas & scripts

An idea board, an idea generator with eight angles per topic and a script workshop that counts the speaking time.

📱

Phone companion

iPhone and Android over your Wi-Fi: capture ideas, tick off posts, enter numbers – no app store needed.

💻

Several PCs

Streaming PC and laptop linked with a code – encrypted through OneDrive, Dropbox or Google Drive, no account at all.

🎨

Your look

Light or dark, nine accent colors, ready-made backgrounds or your own image with an adjustable veil.

🌍

English & German

The app follows your Windows language and can be switched in the settings at any time.

🔒

Local, no account

Your data lives as readable files on your PC. No subscription, no telemetry, daily backups.

🔄

Updates itself

New versions download in the background and install on the next restart – or right away with one click.

🎞️

Media library

Videos and images with tags – and you can see which file is used in which post.

📥

CSV import

Import numbers from the TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and X studios in two clicks – or enter them by hand.

🔔

Reminders

Where a platform can't post by itself, the app reminds you at the scheduled time and puts the finished text on your clipboard.
