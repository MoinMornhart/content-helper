Phone

Content Helper comes with a small app for your phone – no app store and no cloud. The desktop app serves it itself over your Wi-Fi.

Setting up the phone companion

  1. On your PC, open Setup → Phone and turn on the companion.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone's camera – iPhone and Android.
  3. In the browser, choose Add to Home Screen. From then on the companion starts like an app.

What it does

  • Capture ideas when they come – they land on the idea board on your PC.
  • See today and the next few days and tick off posts, including the checklist.
  • Enter numbers for channels that don't sync automatically.
  • Offline: if the PC is off, the phone remembers everything and hands it over later.

The companion follows the language of the desktop app – English or German.

Security

While the companion is off, nothing is reachable from outside. When you turn it on, the app creates a random key that only lives in the QR code; without it, the PC doesn't answer. Create new key locks out every device connected so far.

Calendar subscription

You can subscribe to your plan in Apple Calendar, Outlook or Google Calendar: in the calendar, click Connect calendar. It updates itself there, with a reminder before every post. Alternatively, export it as an .ics file.
