Publishing

Sign in once, then pick a video in the Composer, tick the channels, set a time – the post goes out by itself.

How it works

  1. Sign in – under Setup → Publish, for the platforms you use. The app never sees your password; the platform only gives it a revocable token that stays on your PC.
  2. Pick a video – in the Composer under Video, optionally with a custom thumbnail for YouTube.
  3. Channels and options – each channel shows whether it goes out automatically. YouTube asks for visibility and "made for kids", TikTok for visibility, comments, duets, stitches and commercial content.
  4. Schedule – the app does the rest. You see the status live in the Composer: uploading, scheduled, published – with a link.

When something goes wrong

  • Interrupted upload: the app resumes at the same spot, even after a restart.
  • No connection, platform overloaded: new attempts after 1, 5, 15, 30 and 60 minutes. If YouTube's daily quota is used up, it waits until the next day.
  • Permanent errors such as a missing video file or a revoked sign-in are reported right away with the reason – "Try again" in the Composer.
  • PC was off: missed posts are caught up on the next start, up to one day late. After that the app asks you first.
  • Several PCs: only the PC that also fetches publishes – nothing goes out twice.

To be honest

Every platform only accepts uploads from an app that it has registered and reviewed. Until YouTube and TikTok have reviewed the Content Helper app, uploads land there as "private". X bills every post made through its API individually.

For the publisher: how to create the apps at Google, TikTok, Meta, LinkedIn and X is described step by step in docs/PUBLISHING.md. In the app, Set up opens the right page and fills in whatever you copy there.
